## How to Book

### Step 1: Check Availability

**Online:**
- Visit our booking calendar on the website
- Enter your desired dates
- See real-time availability

**By Phone/Email:**
- Contact us with your preferred dates
- We'll check availability and respond within 24 hours
- Peak season (June-September): Book 2-3 months ahead
- Off-season: Usually available with 1-2 weeks notice

### Step 2: Choose Your Camper

Review our [camper options](campers.md) and let us know which vehicle suits your needs:
- **Classic Adventurer:** Perfect for couples
- **Family Explorer:** Ideal for families or groups

Not sure which to choose? We'll help based on:
- Number of travelers
- Trip duration
- Planned activities
- Budget considerations
- Comfort preferences

### Step 3: Confirm Details

We'll discuss:
- Rental period (minimum 3 days)
- Pickup and drop-off times
- Cross-border travel needs
- Additional equipment
- Special requests
- Insurance options

**Reservation Deposit:**
- 30% of total rental cost
- Payment methods: Bank transfer, credit card, PayPal
- Deposit is non-refundable but transferable to other dates (see policy)

**Final Payment:**
- Due 14 days before pickup
- Payment methods: Bank transfer, credit card, cash (EUR or BGN)
- Security deposit due at pickup (separate from rental payment)

### Step 5: Prepare for Your Adventure

We'll send you:
- Booking confirmation with details
- Pre-trip checklist
- Packing suggestions
- Route planning resources
- Contact information

- Less than 14 days: No refund
- Deposit always transferable to future dates (within 1 year)

### Changes to Booking

**Date Changes:**

**Vehicle Changes:**
- Free upgrade if available
- Downgrade: Refund of difference

We understand that weather and unforeseen circumstances happen:

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Booking & Payments

**Q: When should I book?**
A: Peak season (June-September): 2-3 months ahead. Other times: 2-4 weeks is usually fine.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Bank transfer, credit card (Visa, Mastercard), PayPal, or cash at pickup (EUR/BGN).

**Q: Is the security deposit refundable?**
A: Yes, fully refundable if no damage or violations. Released 7-14 days after return.

**Q: Do you offer discounts?**
A: Yes! Weekly (10%), monthly (25%), repeat customers, and off-season rates.

### Travel & Routes

**Q: Can we take the van outside Bulgaria?**
A: Yes! Most neighboring countries allowed with proper documentation (arranged by us).

**Q: Is wild camping allowed in Bulgaria?**
A: In some areas, yes. We'll provide guidance on where it's permitted and recommended.

**Q: What if we don't speak Bulgarian?**
A: Most tourist areas have English speakers. We provide a phrase guide and translation app suggestions.

**Q: Are the roads safe?**
A: Yes, but mountain roads require careful driving. We'll brief you on what to expect.

### Vehicle & Equipment

**Q: What if something breaks?**
A: Call us immediately. We have 24/7 support and a network of mechanics.

**Q: Can we bring our pet?**
A: Yes, with €30 fee and prior approval. Must be crated or secured while driving.

**Q: Is there WiFi in the van?**
A: Available as optional extra (€7/day). Otherwise, we recommend local SIM cards.

**Q: Can we pick up firewood along the way?**
A: In some areas, but we recommend the camp stove for cooking. Open fires restricted in many places.

### Practical Matters

**Q: What about insurance if we have an accident?**
A: You're covered by our CDW insurance (with excess). We handle the process.

**Q: Where can we empty waste tanks?**
A: Campgrounds, some gas stations, and public dump stations. We provide a list.

**Q: What if we get stuck or lost?**
A: Call us! We have 24/7 support to help with any situation.

**Q: Can we extend our rental while on the road?**
A: If the van isn't booked next, yes! Contact us and we'll arrange it.
