## Our Story

The Stable was born from a simple idea: everyone should experience the freedom and joy of camper van travel. What started as a passion project among friends who loved the outdoors has grown into Bulgaria's trusted camper van rental service.

We're not just a rental company - we're a community of adventurers, nature lovers, and road trip enthusiasts who believe that the best memories are made on the open road.

---

## Our Mission

**To make van life accessible and enjoyable for everyone.**

Whether you're planning your first camping trip or you're a seasoned overlander, we provide:
- Well-maintained, reliable camper vans
- Expert advice and route planning
- 24/7 support throughout your journey
- A welcoming community of fellow travelers
- Comprehensive briefings to ensure your confidence
- Local knowledge from people who truly know Bulgaria

---

## Why "The Stable"?

The name reflects our philosophy: just as horses once carried travelers on great adventures, our camper vans are modern vehicles of exploration and freedom. Like a stable provides care and preparation for the journey ahead, we ensure every traveler is fully equipped and confident before setting out.

Plus, we're based in a beautifully converted stable building - a nod to our rural Bulgarian roots and commitment to preserving local heritage while embracing modern adventure.
