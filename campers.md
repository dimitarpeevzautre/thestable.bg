All our camper vans are meticulously maintained and equipped with everything you need for a comfortable adventure. Each vehicle undergoes thorough inspection before every rental to ensure your safety and peace of mind.

## The Classic Adventurer

**Perfect for couples or solo travelers**

### Specifications
- **Sleeps:** 2 people
- **Transmission:** Manual
- **Fuel:** Diesel
- **Year:** 2018-2020 models

### Interior Features
- **Sleeping Area:** Comfortable double bed with premium mattress
- **Kitchen:** 2-burner gas stove, sink with running water, mini refrigerator (40L)
- **Storage:** Overhead cabinets, under-bed storage, wardrobe
- **Seating:** Dining area that converts to additional lounging space
- **Heating:** Diesel heater for cold nights
- **Electrical:** 12V system, USB charging ports, 230V hookup capability
- **Lighting:** LED interior lighting with dimmer

### Outdoor Equipment
- Awning for shade
- Outdoor table and chairs
- Camping chairs (2)
- Leveling blocks
- Water hose and electrical cable

### Kitchen Equipment Included
- Pots, pans, and cooking utensils
- Plates, bowls, cups, and cutlery for 2
- Coffee maker
- Cutting board and knives
- Dish soap and sponge
- Trash bags

## Vehicle Preparation

Every camper is prepared to the highest standards:

1. **Deep cleaning** between rentals
2. **Mechanical inspection** of all systems
3. **Full fuel tank** at pickup
4. **Fresh water tanks filled**
5. **Waste tanks emptied**
6. **Gas bottles full**
7. **All equipment checked** and restocked
8. **Interior and exterior cleaned**

## What You Need to Drive

- Valid driver's license (held for minimum 2 years)
- Minimum age: 25 years
- Credit card for security deposit
- Valid passport or ID

## Rental Includes

- Unlimited mileage within Bulgaria
- Comprehensive insurance (CDW with excess)
- 24/7 roadside assistance
- Full briefing and handover (approximately 1 hour)
- All kitchen equipment and bedding
- Basic cleaning supplies
- Welcome pack with local tips

## Not Included

- Fuel
- Camping site fees
- Toll roads
- Optional extras

- Cross-border travel permit (if applicable)
