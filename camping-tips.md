# Camping Tips & Advice

[Back to Home](index.md)

Whether you're a first-time camper or a seasoned van-lifer, these tips will help you make the most of your adventure. Learn from our experience and the collective wisdom of countless travelers.

---

## Before You Go

### Essential Pre-Trip Planning

**Research Your Route:**
- Study maps and plan daily distances (max 300km/day recommended)
- Identify camping spots along your route
- Check weather forecasts
- Download offline maps (Maps.me, Google Maps offline)
- Save important locations as favorites

**Document Preparation:**
- Make copies of important documents
- Save emergency numbers in your phone
- Share itinerary with family/friends
- Check visa requirements for cross-border trips
- Verify insurance coverage

**Shopping List:**
- Fresh food for 2-3 days max
- Non-perishable staples
- Drinking water (supplement tanks)
- Basic first aid supplies
- Personal toiletries
- Sunscreen and insect repellent

## Daily Van Life

### Morning Routine

1. **Check levels:** Water, waste, gas, fuel
2. **Secure items:** Ensure nothing will shift while driving
3. **Retract awning** and secure outdoor items
4. **Final walk-around:** Check tires, close all doors/windows
5. **Clean up campsite:** Leave no trace

### Evening Setup

1. **Choose level spot:** Use leveling blocks if needed
2. **Deploy stabilizers** (if equipped)
3. **Connect hook-ups** (if at campground):
   - Electricity (check voltage)
   - Water (use filter)
   - Consider sewage connection
4. **Set up outdoor space:** Awning, chairs, table
5. **Organize interior:** Set up bed, arrange gear

## Driving Tips

### On the Road

**Driving Differences:**
- Higher center of gravity (take turns slower)
- Longer braking distance
- Wider turning radius
- Watch overhead clearances
- Parking requires more space

**Speed Recommendations:**
- Highway: Follow speed limits (max comfort: 100 km/h)
- Mountain roads: Take it slow (40-60 km/h)
- Gravel roads: 30-40 km/h maximum
- Always drive to conditions

**Safety First:**
- Take breaks every 2 hours
- Switch drivers when possible
- Never drive tired
- Be extra cautious in rain/wind
- Pull over if weather is severe

**Parking:**
- Always engage parking brake
- Use wheel chocks on slopes
- Level the van for comfort and fridge efficiency
- Consider noise levels (generators, traffic)
- Respect "No Overnight Parking" signs

## Camping Locations

### Types of Camping

**Organized Campgrounds:**
- Pros: Facilities, safety, electricity, water, community
- Cons: Less privacy, costs, may require booking
- Best for: Families, first-timers, needing full services

**Wild Camping:**
- Pros: Freedom, nature, free, privacy
- Cons: No facilities, may be restricted, requires self-sufficiency
- Best for: Experienced campers, nature lovers

**Important:** Always verify local regulations on wild camping

### Choosing a Spot

**Safety Considerations:**
- Flat, stable ground
- Away from water edges (flash flood risk)
- Not under dead trees
- Exit route available
- Safe neighborhood

**Practical Factors:**
- Level ground (within 5 degrees)
- Drainage (avoid low spots)
- Shade in summer (but sun for solar panels)
- Wind protection
- Scenic views (you're on vacation!)

**Etiquette:**
- Respect quiet hours (usually 10 PM - 7 AM)
- Keep noise levels down
- Respect others' space
- Follow campground rules
- Be friendly but not intrusive

## Water Management

### Fresh Water

**Conservation Tips:**
- Wet dishes first, then turn off water to soap
- Use basin for washing (don't run water continuously)
- Shower at campgrounds when possible
- Use wet wipes between showers
- Capture rinse water for toilet flushing

**Water Sources:**
- Fill at campsites (always ask first)
- Gas stations (some have potable water)
- Public fountains (verify potable)
- Our recommended refill spots (ask us!)

**Storage:**
- Keep tanks 3/4 full (allows expansion)
- Empty and refill every 3-4 days
- Add a few drops of bleach for long-term storage
- Fresh water should taste good (if not, use bottled)

### Grey Water

**Best Practices:**
- Use biodegradable soap
- Strain food particles (compost or trash)
- Never dump on ground (use designated spots)
- Empty when 3/4 full
- Campgrounds have dump stations

## Power Management

### Electrical Systems

**12V System (Leisure Battery):**
- Powers: Lights, water pump, fans, USB chargers
- Recharged by: Driving, solar, hook-up
- Monitor: Battery level indicator
- Conserve: Turn off lights, limit pump use

**230V System (When Plugged In):**
- Powers: Coffee maker, outlets, battery charger
- Only available with electrical hook-up
- Check amperage (usually 6-16A in Europe)
- Don't overload (turn off heater when cooking)

**Solar Panels:**
- Keep panels clean
- Park in sun when possible
- Most effective midday
- Limited output on cloudy days

**Conservation Tips:**
- LED lights use minimal power
- Charge devices while driving
- Use gas for cooking (not electric)
- Limit inverter use
- Turn off unused systems

## Cooking in the Van

### Kitchen Basics

**Gas Safety:**
- Always use ventilation
- Check connections regularly
- Turn off at bottle when not in use
- Never use for heating
- Keep fire extinguisher accessible

**Meal Planning:**
- One-pot meals save water and fuel
- Prep ingredients at home
- Use local markets for fresh ingredients
- Plan meals to minimize waste
- Keep meals simple on travel days

**Easy Camper Van Recipes:**

**Breakfast:**
- Overnight oats (no cooking)
- Eggs any style
- Fresh bread and local cheese
- Coffee or tea

**Lunch:**
- Sandwiches with local ingredients
- Salads prepared in the morning
- Picnic items
- Leftovers from dinner

**Dinner:**
- One-pot pasta
- Stir-fry
- Grilled vegetables and meat
- Rice and beans
- Local traditional foods

**Cooking Tips:**
- Mise en place (prep everything first)
- Use lids to cook faster
- Cook outside when possible
- Share cooking with travel companions
- Try local ingredients

### Food Storage

**Refrigerator:**
- Keep door closed when possible
- Pack efficiently
- Van must be level for proper operation
- Remove batteries from fridges when not in use
- Stock up in larger towns

**Pantry:**
- Use airtight containers
- Secure items against movement
- Rotate stock (FIFO - first in, first out)
- Dry goods: pasta, rice, canned goods
- Spices in small containers

## Weather Considerations

### Hot Weather

**Staying Cool:**
- Park in shade
- Use window shades/reflectors
- Create cross-ventilation
- Stay near water
- Travel to higher elevations
- Avoid midday driving

**Food Safety:**
- Monitor fridge temperature
- Use coolers for extras
- Shop more frequently
- Avoid mayo-based foods
- Keep water bottles frozen

### Cold Weather

**Staying Warm:**
- Use diesel heater efficiently
- Close curtains and shades
- Dress in layers
- Use sleeping bag liners
- Park to catch morning sun
- Insulate windows at night

**Preventing Freezing:**
- Don't let water tanks freeze
- Drain systems if below 0°C overnight
- Use water additives
- Keep gas bottles warm
- Run heater at night

### Rain

**Staying Dry:**
- Check for leaks regularly
- Ventilate to prevent condensation
- Keep doormat inside in rain
- Organize mudroom area
- Dry wet clothes outside when rain stops

**Driving in Rain:**
- Reduce speed significantly
- Increase following distance
- Watch for hydroplaning
- Be cautious of flooded roads
- Pull over if visibility is poor

## Waste Management

### Trash

**Minimize Waste:**
- Choose products with less packaging
- Bring reusable bags and containers
- Separate recyclables
- Compost when possible (where allowed)
- Refuse single-use plastics

**Disposal:**
- Never litter
- Use campground bins
- Separate recycling
- Keep trash bag sealed
- Dispose daily in summer

### Toilet Waste

**Cassette Toilet Care:**
- Add chemical after emptying
- Empty every 2-3 days
- Use designated dump stations
- Rinse thoroughly
- Close blade valve when not in use

**Finding Dump Stations:**
- Campgrounds (ask even if not staying)
- Gas stations (some have facilities)
- Public facilities
- Apps: Park4Night, iOverlander
- Our provided list of locations

## Health & Safety

### First Aid

**Essential Supplies:**
- Bandages and gauze
- Antiseptic wipes
- Pain relievers
- Antihistamines
- Blister treatment
- Tweezers and scissors
- Personal medications
- Sunburn relief

**Common Issues:**
- Blisters: Prevent with proper footwear
- Sunburn: SPF 30+, reapply often
- Insect bites: Repellent, antihistamine
- Cuts/scrapes: Clean and bandage
- Dehydration: Drink regularly, not just when thirsty

### Emergency Situations

**If You Break Down:**
1. Pull over safely
2. Turn on hazard lights
3. Place warning triangle
4. Call roadside assistance
5. Don't attempt repairs on busy roads

**If You're Sick or Injured:**
- EU health card if applicable
- Know location of hospitals
- Emergency number: 112
- Contact us for local recommendations
- Travel insurance info accessible

**If You're Lost:**
- Don't panic
- Check GPS/maps
- Ask locals (friendly people!)
- Return to last known location
- Call us if needed

## Environmental Responsibility

### Leave No Trace Principles

1. **Plan ahead** and prepare
2. **Travel and camp on durable surfaces**
3. **Dispose of waste properly**
4. **Leave what you find**
5. **Minimize campfire impacts** (use camp stove)
6. **Respect wildlife** (observe from distance)
7. **Be considerate** of others

### Eco-Friendly Camping

**Reduce Impact:**
- Use biodegradable soaps
- Minimize single-use plastics
- Conserve water and power
- Support local businesses
- Stay on marked trails
- Don't feed wildlife
- Pack out all trash
- Use existing fire rings only

## Return Preparation

### End of Trip

**Three Days Before Return:**
- Plan return route
- Check fuel levels
- Start using up perishables
- Gather all belongings
- Check for damage

**Day Before Return:**
- Empty waste tanks
- Fill fresh water
- Clean interior
- Refuel completely
- Remove trash

**Return Day:**
- Allow 1 hour for handover
- Return with full fuel tank
- Clean and tidy (basic cleaning, we do deep clean)
- Replace any used consumables
- Report any issues honestly

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Overpacking:** Less is more in a van
2. **Driving too much:** Enjoy the journey
3. **Not leveling properly:** Affects sleep and fridge
4. **Forgetting to lower antenna/awning:** Expensive damage
5. **Running out of water:** Always keep tank half full
6. **Overloading electrical system:** Know your limits
7. **Not planning for weather:** Check forecasts
8. **Skipping vehicle checks:** Prevent problems
9. **Being antisocial:** Community is half the fun
10. **Rushing:** Slow down and enjoy!
